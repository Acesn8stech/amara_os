## 4. Top-lease and subordination
To the extent any prior lease is later found to remain in force, the lease taken hereunder is a top lease: it attaches automatically, without further instrument, at the moment the prior lease terminates or expires. Optionee takes no action that would extend or ratify the prior lease. Owner makes no representation that the prior lease has expired; Optionee relies on its own examination.

## 5. Assignability
Optionee may assign this option and any resulting lease in whole or part without Owner's consent, with notice to Owner. Assignor is released as to obligations accruing after assignment.
