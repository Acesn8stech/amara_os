## 1. Services
Contractor will pull and abstract the instruments listed on each COURTHOUSE_FIELD_TICKET issued by Company (certified copies, marginal references, indices) in {{county}} County and any other county named on a ticket, and return a written abstract in the Runs Sheet format Company provides. Contractor works only on tickets accepted in writing.

## 2. Independent contractor
Contractor controls the manner and means of the work, supplies their own tools and vehicle, may work for others, and is responsible for their own taxes and insurance. No employee benefits accrue.

## 3. Licensure and standards
Contractor represents they hold every license, bond or registration {{state}} requires to abstract title, and will follow AAPL title examination standards. The abstract is a record summary, not a title opinion; Contractor must not render legal opinions.

## 4. Fees and costs
Fee per ticket and approved out-of-pocket costs (copies, recording fees, mileage) are as stated on the accepted ticket. Costs must be supported by receipts; Company books them to the tract ledger. Unreceipted costs are not reimbursable.

## 5. Confidentiality
All tract identities, owner information, pricing, buyers and Company's methods are confidential. Use only for the services; return or destroy on request. Survives 3 years after termination (trade secrets: as long as they remain secret).

## 6. Non-circumvent
For 24 months Contractor will not, directly or indirectly, contact any owner, operator or buyer identified through the services to lease, buy, or broker interests in the tracts, nor assist others in doing so, except as Company directs. Remedies include injunctive relief and fees; if a court narrows this clause, it is enforced to the extent allowed by {{state}} law.

## 7. Accuracy and E&O
Contractor warrants the abstract accurately reflects the records examined and will carry errors-and-omissions coverage of at least the amount stated on the accepted ticket.

## 8. General
Governed by {{state}} law; sole agreement on this subject; amendments in writing; e-signatures valid.
