## 1. Reserved overriding royalty
Assignor reserves an overriding royalty interest equal to **{{orri_pct}}** of 8/8ths of all oil, gas and other minerals produced and saved from the Lands, proportionately reduced only to the extent the Lease covers less than the full mineral estate (the "ORRI"). The ORRI is free of cost of production, and is borne by Assignee's working interest only, delivered on the same basis as the lessor royalty.

## 2. Anti-washout
The ORRI attaches to every extension, renewal, ratification, or new lease of any part of the Lands taken by Assignee, its affiliates, successors or assigns within **one (1) year after the Lease expires or terminates**, and to any lease taken in substitution for the Lease. No surrender, release or non-renewal may be used to defeat the ORRI.

## 3. Pooling
If the Lease is pooled or unitized, the ORRI is computed on the production allocated to the Lease. Assignee may not pool the Lease in a manner that reduces the ORRI other than proportionately to acreage.

## 4. Covenants
Assignee will pay the ORRI directly to Assignor and furnish division-order and run-statement information. Assignor warrants only that it has not previously assigned or encumbered the Lease; assignment is otherwise without warranty of title, as is standard for lease flips.

## 5. Burden on successors
The ORRI is a covenant running with the Lease and binds assigns. Any assignment by Assignee must expressly be made subject to this instrument. Recordable.
